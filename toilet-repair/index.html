Toilet Overflowing? Do This First

Reach behind the toilet and turn the small supply valve clockwise to stop the refill. If water is still rising, lift the tank lid and push the flapper down to stop flow into the bowl. Call (855) 575-2890 to talk through the problem and ask about timing.

Do not flush again. Put towels at the base and keep the bathroom door closed if sewage has reached the floor. If other drains are also slow, the blockage may be in the main line rather than the toilet.

Why Toilets Fail in Beach Homes

Rental bathrooms see heavy and varied use. Visitors flush things that do not belong in a toilet, such as wipes, cotton products and toys, and the trap holds them until a clog forms. Sand and grit also wear the flapper seat and fill valve, which makes toilets run on or refill by themselves.

Humidity and salt air corrode closet bolts and supply lines near the Gulf and the bay. A wobbly toilet that rocks at the base needs attention, since movement breaks the wax seal and lets water seep into the subfloor.

When a Toilet Clogs Again and Again

One clog is a nuisance. Repeated clogs mean something else is going on, such as a partial blockage past the trap, a low-flow toilet that is poorly matched to the drain slope or a main line problem. If a tub and toilet back up together, that is a main line signal and a cable or camera is the next step.

Our drain cleaning team can clear the line and check it on camera if the pattern continues. A damaged toilet flange can also cause leaks and odors and should be repaired rather than patched.

Repair or Replace?

Parts are cheap, so a running toilet or a weak flush is usually worth repairing. A cracked bowl, a leaking tank seam or a toilet that has needed repeated work may be better replaced. If you are replacing, choose a model with a rough-in that matches your flange and a flush rating suited to your drain.

We install and set new toilets, replace wax rings and supply lines and check the floor for damage. If we find rot around the flange, we tell you before closing things up.

Condos and Rentals

Toilet leaks cause downstairs damage in condos, and association rules often require a licensed plumber. We document the repair and give you a written summary for the manager. For rental owners, a quick bathroom check between guests, looking for rocking and base moisture, prevents most surprises. Related work on fixtures is covered on our faucet repair page.
