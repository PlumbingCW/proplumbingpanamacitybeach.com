Sewage Backing Up? What to Do Before the Plumber Arrives

Call (855) 575-2890 to talk through the problem and ask about timing. Stop using every toilet, sink, shower and washer, because each use sends more water toward the damaged section. Keep people and pets away from any wet floor and wear gloves if you must touch wet material.

If water is pooling in the yard near a cleanout, keep children off that area. Do not run the dishwasher or start a laundry load to see if the problem clears. It will not, and it adds to the mess inside.

Signs the Sewer Line Itself Is Damaged

A clogged branch drain affects one fixture. A damaged main affects the whole house, and the signs repeat even after a cleaning.

  • Backups that return within weeks of a cable or jetting visit
  • Soft, sunken or unusually green patches in the yard along the line
  • Sewage smell outdoors near the cleanout or foundation
  • Slow drains in every bathroom at once
  • Gurgling from a toilet when another fixture drains

What Damages Sewer Lines in Bay County

Older homes in Panama City and parts of Lynn Haven were built with clay, cast iron or fiber pipe, and those materials corrode, crack and separate at joints with age. Roots follow moisture, and a small joint gap is enough for them to enter. Sandy coastal soil also shifts and settles, which can open a joint or create a belly where waste collects.

Storms add to it. Hurricane Michael came ashore in the Panhandle in October 2018, and repair and rebuilding work in Bay County has left some properties with older pipe joined to newer runs. Those transitions are a common place for leaks, so a camera pass looks closely at them. See plumber services in Panama City for nearby housing notes.

How We Diagnose the Break

A repair plan starts with a camera. The camera runs through the cleanout and records the pipe, showing roots, cracks, offsets, bellies and collapses. A locator then marks the depth and position at the surface, so any digging is aimed at one spot instead of a trench across the yard.

We use that footage to talk through the options with you. If you want to see the footage first, the sewer inspection page explains what is recorded and what you receive.

Repair Options and the Trade-Offs

Sewer repair options compared
OptionBest forTrade-off
Spot repairOne cracked or offset sectionNeeds excavation at that point
Full replacementPipe that is failing along its lengthMore excavation and a longer job
Pipe liningSound alignment with cracks or root entryNot suited to collapsed or badly bellied pipe
Pipe burstingReplacing a line with limited diggingNeeds access pits and suitable soil

Lining, also called cured-in-place pipe, places a resin-coated liner inside the old pipe and cures it into a new pipe within the old one. It saves driveways and landscaping when the pipe still holds its shape. It does not fix a collapse, and a camera is the only honest way to tell.

Permits, Locates and Your Property

Digging near a sewer lateral means checking for buried utilities first. Florida requires a call to 811 before excavation, and we handle that step. Permit rules for sewer work vary, so we ask the Panama City Beach building department what applies to your address instead of guessing.

If the damage is on the city side of the property line, the repair is the municipality’s responsibility. We can show you on camera where the break sits relative to the cleanout so you know who to call.
