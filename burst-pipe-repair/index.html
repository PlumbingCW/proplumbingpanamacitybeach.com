Pipe Burst? What to Do Right Now

Shut off the main water valve right away. It is usually near the water meter, at the front of the house or where the line enters the garage. Turn it clockwise until it stops. Call (855) 575-2890 to talk through the problem and ask about timing.

Next, switch off the breakers for wet rooms if you can reach the panel safely. Open a faucet at the lowest point to drain remaining water. Move valuables up and out of the wet area and photograph the damage for your insurer before cleanup.

What Causes Pipes to Burst on the Gulf Coast

Freezing is less common here than in northern states, but occasional hard freezes do reach the Panhandle, and exposed pipes, outdoor spigots and unheated crawlspaces are the pieces that split. Insulating those items before a cold night is cheap compared to the repair.

Most bursts in this area come from other causes. Corroded copper, failing fittings, high water pressure, a stuck pressure regulator and ground movement all break lines. Plastic fittings that age in sunlight, such as outdoor PEX left uncovered, also fail. Every cause has a different fix and a different warning sign.

Signs a Pipe Is About to Fail

  • Water pressure that seems too high, with banging pipes
  • Green or white crust on copper joints
  • Damp spots on drywall near a plumbing wall
  • Discolored water after a long idle period
  • Repeated small leaks on the same line

A pressure gauge on a hose bib shows whether pressure is too high. If the house runs well above the typical residential range, a pressure reducing valve is a modest repair that protects every pipe in the house.

What We Do on the Emergency Visit

  1. Confirm the main is off and find the damaged section
  2. Drain the line and open the access needed to reach the break
  3. Replace the damaged section with proper pipe and fittings
  4. Pressure test the line before closing anything up
  5. Explain what failed and whether other sections look at risk

If the same line has leaked repeatedly, a broken section may be one symptom of a pipe that is failing everywhere. That is when repiping is worth pricing against another patch. For a leak with no visible burst, see leak detection.

After the Repair: Drying and Damage

Plumbing stops the water. Drying and restoration come next. Wet drywall, insulation and flooring need to dry or be removed within a day or two to avoid mold. We can tell you what we saw and where the water went, and a water mitigation company can handle extraction and drying.

For rental owners, a burst pipe between guests is a different problem than one during a stay. Call us first, call the guests second, and document the timeline. A written record helps with insurance and guest communication.
