Where Hombre and The Glades Sit

The Glades is a residential neighborhood off Panama City Beach Parkway, with single-family homes on quiet streets such as Hombre Circle. Caballeros Estates at Hombre is a D.R. Horton community at 101 Dama Del Mar Way that the builder describes as next to the established Glades neighborhood.

D.R. Horton gives directions from the Hathaway Bridge: west on Panama City Beach Parkway, left on North Glades Trail, right on Hombre Circle, then right on Coyote Pass. Homes there include detached houses and townhomes. These addresses use Panama City Beach and ZIP 32407.

The Hombre Golf Club Connection

The name Hombre comes from the Hombre Golf Club at 120 Coyote Pass. It opened in 1989 with 27 holes designed by Wes Burnham and closed in 2018 after Hurricane Michael damage. Many residents still use the name for the neighborhood.

A closed golf course says nothing about your pipes. It is a landmark that helps a plumber find the right street, and it explains why the area has both older and brand new homes close together.

Two Eras, Two Different Starting Points

The Glades homes predate the newer Caballeros Estates, but this page cannot give a build year for each street. Look up your parcel on the Bay County Property Appraiser site to see the year built before assuming anything about the pipes.

Common Calls for Homes Like These

Single-family homes need the same core plumbing work wherever they sit: clearing a stopped drain, repairing or replacing a water heater, finding a leak and replacing worn fixtures. Start with drain cleaning if a sink or tub is slow, and leak detection if the bill is up and nothing is visibly wet.

When a water heater fails, water heater replacement covers sizing and the choice between tank and tankless. For toilets, faucets and supply lines, see toilet repair and faucet repair.

Before the Plumber Arrives

1

Find the main shutoff

Know where the home's main water valve is and turn it by hand once so you know it moves.

2

Note the symptom

Write down which fixtures are affected, when it started and any sounds.

3

Photograph labels

Take photos of the water heater label and any fixture model numbers.

4

Clear the access

Move items away from the water heater, the cleanout and under-sink cabinets.

City Limits and Utilities

Panama City Beach addresses are not always inside the city limits. Check your tax district on the Bay County Property Appraiser site, where city properties show City of Panama City Beach, District 13. The Utilities Department says its water, sewer and reclaimed water franchise area extends beyond the city limits, so ask Utilities about your own street.
