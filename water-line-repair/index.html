Water Line Broken? Do This Now

Call (855) 575-2890 to talk through the problem and ask about timing. Shut off the valve at the house if you can reach it. If water is surfacing in the yard, mark the spot with a cone or chair and keep children and pets away from soft ground. The valve at the meter belongs to the utility, so do not force it.

If the whole house has no water, check whether neighbors are affected. A neighborhood outage is a utility matter. If only your home is dry or a wet patch appeared, the problem is likely on your side of the meter and we can help.

Where the Line Runs and Who Owns It

The service line connects the water main at the street to your house, passing through the meter. The utility generally maintains the main and the meter, and you maintain the line on your side. Where that division sits depends on the utility, so we confirm it before any repair.

That matters because utilities draw the ownership line in different places. If a leak is upstream of the meter, we help you document it for the utility. If it is downstream, we repair it.

Signs of a Failing Service Line

  • A soggy or green strip of yard with no rain or sprinkler explanation
  • Low pressure throughout the house
  • A meter that runs with every fixture off
  • The sound of hissing near the meter or the front hose bib
  • A high bill with no change in use

The meter test on our leak detection page is the fastest first step. It confirms water is leaving the system and guides the next move.

Repair, Replacement and Trenchless Choices

Spot repairOne break on an otherwise sound lineNeeds a small excavation

Trenchless replacementLines under drives, walks or landscapingNeeds suitable soil and access pits

Older Panama City neighborhoods may still have galvanized or early copper service lines that scale and pinhole. In newer areas, plastic lines can fail at fittings or from ground shifts. We look at what is in the ground before recommending anything.

Locates, Permits and Backfill

Before digging, we call 811 so buried gas, electric and communications lines are marked. We also ask the building department and the water utility which permits and inspections apply to your address. Good backfill and compaction keep the trench from settling later.

In sandy coastal soil, backfill settles if it is not compacted in layers. We restore the trench and tell you what to expect for grass and hardscape afterward.

Pressure, Valves and Related Work

A new service line is a good time to check the pressure reducing valve and the main shutoff, since both live near where the line enters the house. A failed regulator can push pressure high enough to damage fixtures and trigger a burst pipe later.
