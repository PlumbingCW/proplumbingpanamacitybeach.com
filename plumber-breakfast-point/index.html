About Breakfast Point and Breakfast Point East

Breakfast Point is a residential community in Panama City Beach developed by The St. Joe Company. St. Joe has described the original community as a sold out development of 449 homesites. Next to it, Breakfast Point East was planned for more than 700 homesites built in phases.

The first phase of Breakfast Point East covered 235 homesites. Site work began in 2021 and the first homesites closed in December 2021. A 2023 builder announcement describes East as a year-round residential neighborhood, not a vacation rental community. The City of Panama City Beach also names the Breakfast Point subdivision as the end point of its Gayle's Trails multi-use trail.

What Younger Homes Change About Plumbing Calls

A house whose first closing was in the last several years is not dealing with decades of wear. Problems tend to be about installation details, fixture quality, water chemistry and settings, not age. That shifts the first question from "what is old" to "what changed".

Check warranty firstA builder or manufacturer warranty may still cover a defect. Read it before authorizing a repair, and ask what the warranty requires.
Look for the patternOne dripping faucet is a fixture issue. Several fixtures with low pressure or hammering point at the supply or a pressure setting.
Document the issueDated photos and the model numbers of the water heater and fixtures help any warranty claim or insurance report.
Ask what is coveredAsk the builder which items stay with them and which go to the manufacturer.

Water Heaters in a Young Home

Most tank water heaters and tankless units in a house this recent are still early in their service life. Routine care matters more than replacement. Tank units benefit from periodic flushing and an anode rod check, and tankless units are descaled on the schedule the manufacturer sets.

If a unit shuts down, shows an error code or runs cold, note the code and model before you call. See water heater repair for how a diagnosis works, or tankless water heaters if yours is a tankless model.

Slow Drains, Leaks and Hidden Water

A slow drain in a recent home is often a local blockage from hair, wipes or kitchen grease. A camera look makes sense when the same line stops twice. Read about drain cleaning for the usual steps from a cable to a camera.

If your water bill jumps and nothing is visibly wet, the water meter can tell you whether water is moving when everything is off. Leak detection explains how a plumber confirms a hidden leak before any wall is opened.

Where City Responsibility Ends

The City of Panama City Beach Utilities Department maintains water, sewer and reclaimed water lines in city rights of way and dedicated easements. Where that responsibility ends and the homeowner's begins at your lot is not something this page can state, so ask Utilities before you pay for a repair near the street or the meter.

Before any digging in your yard, call 811 so buried lines are marked. For a suspected broken or clogged public line, the city directs residents to its SeeClickFix reporting app on the Utilities Department page.

Is Your Address Inside the City?

Not every Panama City Beach address sits inside the city limits. The city says you can check by searching your parcel on the Bay County Property Appraiser site, where a city property shows the tax district as City of Panama City Beach, District 13. This matters for permits and for who answers building questions.
