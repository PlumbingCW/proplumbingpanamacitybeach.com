What to Do While You Wait

2

If a toilet gurgles or the tub fills, stop using water until the line is open.

3

4

Yards, Trees and the Lines Underneath

Mature oaks and pines send roots toward sewer joints, and a hairline gap is enough for them to enter. A line that clogs every year or two usually has a root entry point. Clearing the line gives temporary relief, and a lining or a joint repair gives lasting relief. See sewer line repair for the choices.

Irrigation systems are common on larger lots, and a leaking valve or buried line can look like a soft patch of lawn. A meter check tells you whether it is a plumbing leak or a sprinkler leak.

After Storms and Power Outages

Hurricane Michael left lasting damage across Bay County in 2018, and some Lynn Haven homes have since been repaired or rebuilt. After any major storm, check for sewer smells, gurgling drains and wet spots, since shifted soil and fallen trees can damage buried lines. Photos and a camera scope help with insurance claims.

Power outages also affect sewage ejector pumps and sump pumps, where they exist. If a pump failed, call and describe what you see so we can plan the visit.

Older Homes and Newer Subdivisions

Lynn Haven mixes older homes near the original streets with newer subdivisions that have grown over the past decades. Older houses may have cast iron, clay or galvanized lines, and newer ones usually use PVC and PEX. The failure patterns differ, so we ask the age of the home on the call and plan equipment around it.
