About Colony Club

The City of Panama City Beach names the Colony Club Subdivision on its Gayle's Trails project page. The new 12-foot-wide asphalt trail runs from east of Colony Club to the Breakfast Point subdivision. The city received a state trail grant in November 2018 and approved a construction contract in February 2023.

A local realtor site describes Colony Club as a quiet, established neighborhood of mostly custom-built single-family homes, many with large yards and private pools. It says there are no HOA fees. This page treats that as a description from one source, so confirm anything that affects a purchase or a repair with the property records.

Why Larger Lots and Pools Change the Plumbing

A custom home on a large lot usually has more outdoor water than a condo: hose bibs, irrigation, outdoor showers and pool equipment. Each adds a place for a leak to hide and a reason for the water meter to keep moving.

Pool supply and fill linesLeaks in a fill line or an automatic filler can run for weeks without a visible puddle.
Irrigation linesA broken irrigation line can raise the water bill and soak one corner of the yard.
Outdoor spigotsWorn hose bibs drip behind shrubs where no one looks.
Longer service linesA longer run from the meter to the house has more joints that can fail.

Finding a Hidden Outdoor Leak

A simple meter test is the first step. Turn off every fixture and appliance, then watch the meter or its leak indicator. If it keeps moving, water is escaping somewhere between the meter and the fixtures.

A plumber can then split the system with valves to narrow the zone. See leak detection for the tools used, and water line repair when the problem is in the service line between the meter and the house. Irrigation and pool-side failures are often a separate trade from the house plumbing, so ask who repairs which part.

Water Heaters and Indoor Plumbing

Larger custom homes can have more than one water heater or a long hot water run to a distant bathroom. Long waits for hot water and uneven temperatures are worth describing carefully on the call. See water heater repair and water heater installation for what affects sizing and placement.

Drains and Sewer Lines

Mature landscaping is a common source of trouble for buried lines because roots seek moisture. If a drain backs up more than once, a camera look shows whether roots, a belly or a joint problem is the cause. Read about sewer camera inspection before deciding on a repair.

Utilities and Permits

The City Utilities Department maintains water, sewer and reclaimed water lines in city rights of way and easements, and its franchise area extends beyond city limits. To confirm whether a parcel is inside the city, check the tax district on the Bay County Property Appraiser site. Ask the local building department which permits apply before any water line or heater work.
