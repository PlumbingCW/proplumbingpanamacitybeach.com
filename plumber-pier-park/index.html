About the Pier Park Area

Pier Park opened on February 14, 2008 as an outdoor lifestyle center on a single level of about 895,974 square feet. Its listed tenants include Dillard's, J.C. Penney and Target as anchors, with 141 stores in all, a movie theater and a number of restaurants. Pier Park North was completed in 2015, and Pier Park West is an adjacent strip of shops.

The City of Panama City Beach created the Front Beach Road Community Redevelopment Area in June 2001. The city lists about 14 streets in it, including Front Beach Road, Alf Coleman Road, Churchwell Drive, Hutchison Boulevard, South Thomas Drive and South Arnold Road.

Plumbing in a Restaurant and Retail District

Commercial plumbing is not residential plumbing at a larger scale. Kitchens, restrooms, floor drains and large water heaters carry more load, and a failure can close a business. Most of what follows is general information, not a statement about any one tenant.

Grease and kitchen drainsFats and oils collect in kitchen drain lines and traps. A line that is slow after the dinner rush is a warning, not a one-off.
Floor drainsFloor drains in kitchens and mechanical rooms can back up when a main line slows. They also dry out when unused and let odor in.
Restroom fixturesHigh-traffic restrooms wear flush valves, toilets and faucets faster than a house would.
Water heatersRestaurants and hotels need hot water at peak hours, so sizing and recovery matter as much as the equipment itself.

Hydro Jetting and Maintenance Planning

When grease has built up in a line, a cable may open a hole through it without cleaning the pipe wall. Hydro jetting cleans the full diameter of a line with high-pressure water, and a camera first confirms the pipe is sound enough for it.

A planned cleaning during a slow week costs the business less than an emergency cleaning on a busy night. See drain cleaning and commercial plumbing for the services that fit a recurring schedule.

Backflow and Code Items to Ask About

Many commercial properties have backflow prevention devices that must be tested on a schedule. Rules for devices, testing and grease control are set by the water utility and the local building department, so ask both what applies to your address. Work follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing.

Hotels and Condo Buildings Nearby

The area around Pier Park and along Front Beach Road also includes hotels and condo buildings. Shared risers and stacks serve several units, and an association often controls shutoff times and contractor requirements. A leak that appears in one unit may start in another, so leak detection is often the first step.

Questions to Ask Before You Hire

  • Is the plumber licensed, and can you verify it on the state lookup?
  • Who pulls the permit, and what inspection follows?
  • What is the plan if the work blocks a kitchen or a restroom?
  • How will the work be documented for the landlord or association?
